Game Day & Arrival

Address & Directions

The ballpark is located at 1500 Melbourne Rd, Charlottesville, VA 22901. Detailed driving directions can be found using the Google Map below.

Parking

Free parking is available on-site. Please follow the signs and instructions from our parking staff to find designated fan parking areas.

Gates Open

Gates open to the public one (1) hour prior to the scheduled first pitch.

Rain Policy / Rain Checks

In the event of a rainout, your ticket can be exchanged for a ticket of equal or lesser value to any remaining home game. Please check our website and social media for the latest updates on game status during inclement weather.

Tickets & Seating

Tickets

Tickets can be purchased online through our website or at the ballpark entry on game days. The field opens one (1) hour before first pitch.

Seating Policy

Ballpark Rules & Prohibited Items

Alcoholic Beverages

No outside alcoholic beverages are permitted. Alcohol is not available for sale at our home games.

Bags

To ensure fan safety, all bags are subject to search upon entry.

Bottles and Cans

Prohibited Items

Prohibited items include, but are not limited to: weapons of any kind, alcoholic beverages, coolers, and laser pointers. A full list can be obtained upon request.

Smoking Policy

The ballpark is a smoke-free facility. Smoking, including e-cigarettes and vaping, is not permitted on school grounds.

Amenities & Guest Services

Animals

Certified service animals are welcome at the ballpark. All other pets are not permitted, except on designated "Bark in the Park" promotional nights.

Concessions

Our concession stands offer a variety of options, including burgers, fries, snacks, cold beverages, & Ben and Jerrys Ice Cream. Credit cards are accepted.

First Aid

A first aid station is available if needed. If you need immediate medical assistance, please contact the nearest staff member.

Lost and Found

Merchandise

Official Tom Sox gear is available at the merchandise tent located near the field. The store is open from the time gates open until the end of the 7th inning.

Fan Experience & On-Field Action

Autographs

Players may be available to sign autographs along the railing near the dugouts during specific times and games. Please be respectful of players as they prepare for the game.

Foul Balls

Fans are encouraged to please return foul ball hit into the stands or grass. Exchange them at the concession stand for a treat. Please be aware and stay alert for balls leaving the field of play at all times.

On-Field Access

Access to the field is restricted to authorized personnel only. Any fan entering the field of play without permission will be subject to immediate ejection.
