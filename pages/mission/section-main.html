Our Mission

Creating an Unparalleled Summer Baseball Experience

The Charlottesville Tom Sox (Charlottesville Community Baseball, Inc.) are a non-profit organization focused on impacting the Charlottesville community by providing a world-class summer baseball experience for the best players across the country.

Our mission is to create an unparalleled summer collegiate baseball experience through the development of our players and volunteers and engagement in and with our community, and by holding ourselves to standards of excellence and professionalism on and off the field.

Our Vision

To be an integral asset in the Charlottesville community for our fans, players, and volunteers.

The Tom Sox Legacy

Excellence isn't a goal—it's our standard.

We are committed to an elite on-field product that has produced 7 South Division Titles and 4 Valley League Championships since our inception in 2015. Throughout the summer, players travel all across Central Virginia during the season and reside with local host families who generously open up their homes to these elite athletes.

As a premier collegiate summer baseball team, we are not just about the game – we're about fostering a culture of integrity, sportsmanship, and growth both on and off the field.

The DEEP Philosophy

Our core values guide everything we do, shaping champions and strengthening our community.

D

Development

We strive to develop our players and volunteers to become productive members of society in life beyond the game of baseball.

  • We set high standards for ourselves, striving for excellence in every endeavor.
  • Through continuous improvement and learning, players, staff, and board members are empowered to reach their full potential, both personally and professionally, fostering a culture of growth and development within the Tom Sox family.
E

Engagement

We engage our community to provide unique opportunities for youth development through relationships with our players and volunteers, and make a difference in the lives of the people of Charlottesville.

  • We are deeply rooted in the community, actively engaging in local initiatives and supporting causes that matter.
  • From volunteering to community outreach events, our organization prioritizes making a positive impact beyond the baseball diamond, strengthening our connection with the community we serve.
E

Excellence

We hold ourselves to high standards of conduct to win on and off the field.

  • Upholding honesty, transparency, and ethical behavior is at the core of our organization.
  • Players, staff, and board members are expected to embody integrity in all aspects of their involvement with the Tom Sox, fostering a culture of trust and accountability.
P

Passion

We care deeply about this organization and maximizing the impact we can make through exciting summer entertainment for the whole family.

  • Embracing good sportsmanship and fostering a culture of teamwork are fundamental to our success.
  • On and off the field, we prioritize mutual support, collaboration, and respect, creating an environment where every member of the Tom Sox family feels valued and empowered to succeed.
