Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Tom Sox Summer Camp

What should my child bring to camp?

Campers should come wearing sneakers (or cleats) and a hat with their baseball glove and a refillable water bottle. Cleats, helmets, and bats are welcome and encouraged. Water and Gatorade will be provided along with shade breaks. Please label all personal items with your child's name and apply sunscreen before drop-off.

What if my child has never played baseball before?

No experience necessary! Our camp is designed for all skill levels, from first-time players to experienced athletes. Coaches group campers appropriately and tailor instruction to meet each child where they are.

Where is camp held?

Camp is held at CHO Airport Field at Charlottesville High School, the home field of the Charlottesville Tom Sox.

What happens in bad weather?

Camp continues rain or shine, but not during lightning or extreme heat. In the event of inclement weather, notifications will go out by email and text as soon as possible.

Will there be snacks or lunch provided?

We do not provide food, but campers are welcome to bring their own. Camp ends at noon before lunch. Water and Gatorade will be provided along with shade breaks. Please plan to hydrate campers well in the days before and during camp, and inform us of any known allergies during registration.

How do I get the host family discount?

Host families may attend any camp session for free using their unique discount code. Contact your host family coordinator to receive your code.
