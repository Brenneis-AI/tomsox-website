A Legacy in the Making: The Tom Sox Story

July 2013

The Idea is Born

After discussions with local "baseball people," Charlottesville businessman Jeff Burton meets with Valley League Commissioner Don Lemish about bringing a team to Charlottesville.

November 2013

It's Official!

The Valley League member teams vote to award a franchise to Charlottesville, set to begin play in the 2015 season.

November 2014

The Tom Sox are Born

After a public contest with 266 names, "Tom Sox" is chosen. The team colors are announced as blue, green, and gray at a press conference.

June 4, 2015

First Game in History

The Tom Sox take the field for the first time, falling 4-2. Brett Johnson scores the first run in franchise history.

August 8, 2017

First Valley League Championship!

In just their third year, the Tom Sox triumph 2-1 over Strasburg to win the title. Michael Wielansky is named league MVP with a record .428 batting average.

August 5, 2018

Back to the Championship

The Tom Sox advance to the Valley League Championship for the second-straight season, defeating the Lumberjacks 7-6.

June 4, 2019

100th All-Time Win

The Tom Sox win their 100th all-time game, a 12-7 victory over Winchester. Dan Harwood ties a team record with five RBI.

August 7, 2019

Back on Top: Second Championship

The team advances to the championship for the third straight year and brings home the hardware, defeating the Strasburg Express 4-1.

April 2, 2020

Season Canceled

Due to the COVID-19 Pandemic, the Valley Baseball League cancels the 2020 season.

June 5, 2021

The Return to Play

The Tom Sox return to the field for the first time in two years, defeating the Covington Lumberjacks 9-5.

June 27, 2022

The First Tom Sox in the MLB!

2017 Tom Sox player Vinnie Pasquantino is called up to the Kansas City Royals, becoming the first Tom Sox alum to play in Major League Baseball.

August 3, 2022

A Three-Time Dynasty!

The Tom Sox hoist the cup for the third time in five seasons, finishing with a perfect 6-0 playoff run.

June 18, 2023

200th All-Time Win

Charlottesville wins their 200th all-time game with an 8-6 triumph over the Culpeper Cavaliers.

August 1, 2024

Four Rings, Seven Seasons

The Tom Sox cement their dynasty, winning their fourth Valley League Championship by defeating the Purcellville Cannons 2-1.
